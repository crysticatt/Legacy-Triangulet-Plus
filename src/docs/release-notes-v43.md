## Triangulet Plus v43 (formerly Triangulet Opener)

**New**
- **Renamed to Triangulet Plus.** The app, installer, shortcuts and files all say Triangulet Plus now. Your login, settings and stats carry over, and old versions can still update to this one.
- **Custom themes.** Settings > Custom themes (Windows) or the Theme section (Mac dashboard): pick four colours (background, cards, accent, text), name it and save. Up to 12 themes.
- **"made by crysticat"** credit in the bottom right of the main screen.

**Fixed**
- **Least and Wish modes could not be selected.** A settings check reset them back to "Next". They stick now.
- **"Login failed: rejected, wrong username" even with the right login.** The first login address/field tried no longer ends the search; more field names are tried (name, username, user, email). The window now shows what the site actually said, and a plain 403 block is no longer called a wrong password.

**Download**: Windows `TrianguletPlus-Setup-v43.exe` (installer) or `TrianguletPlus-v43.exe` (portable), Mac `TrianguletPlus-Mac-v43.zip`. Upload the `TrianguletOpener-v43.exe` / `TrianguletOpener-mac-*-v43` copies too so older versions auto-update.
