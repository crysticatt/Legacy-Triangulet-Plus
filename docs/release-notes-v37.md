## Triangulet Opener v37

**New**
- **Settings** button (bottom left): theme (Dark, Light, Blue), update controls, phone alerts, backup.
- **Phone alerts** through the free ntfy app: rare pulls and warnings on your phone.
- **Encrypted backup**: export and import your settings, logins and History with a passphrase.
- **Update controls**: check now, turn the check off, optionally install updates by itself while hidden. A "what's new" box shows these notes after an update.
- **Password protection**: the saved password is encrypted (Windows) or in the Keychain (Mac).

**Downloads**
- Windows: `TrianguletOpener-Setup-v37.exe` (installer) or `TrianguletOpener-v37.exe` (portable)
- Mac: `TrianguletOpener-Mac-v37.zip`
