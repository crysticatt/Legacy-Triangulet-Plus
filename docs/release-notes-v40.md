## Triangulet Opener v40

**New**
- **Luck tab**: how long your first full collection will take (packs and time, per capsule), and how lucky you've been (Mystical / Legendary / Epic / Rare vs the site's odds). Has a **Copy diagnostics** button for bug reports (no passwords or tokens).
- **Smarter hunting**: Hunt now blends the site's odds with the odds measured from your own pulls (marked with `*` on Windows).
- **Downtime hours**: in Settings, type the hours the opener should stay *paused*, e.g. `3:20pm-4pm, 2am-2:30am`. It pauses by itself and resumes when the window ends. The status pill shows "Paused".

**Fixed**
- Auction: you can pick a trian again (Windows).

**Download**: Windows `TrianguletOpener-Setup-v40.exe`, Mac `TrianguletOpener-Mac-v40.zip`.
